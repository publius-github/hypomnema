Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Hypomnema is a local-first planner, journal and notes app for Windows and Android, made by <COPYRIGHT HOLDER> ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we never receive your data. There is no Hypomnema server, no account with us, no analytics and no advertising.

Data that stays on your device

Everything you write - tasks, lists, journal entries, notes, settings and the configuration files - is stored as plain files and a local SQLite database in a folder you choose (your "vault"). The app also keeps, on the device only:

  • a random device identifier, used to tell your devices' edits apart when they are synced;
  • if you sign in to Google: your Google account's email address, name and Drive user id (to show who is signed in), and on Windows the OAuth tokens that let the app reach your Drive. On Android, Google Play services keeps those tokens instead. On Windows you can seal the tokens with a passphrase.

We have no access to any of this.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app syncs your vault between your own devices through your own Google Drive. It asks for a single permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, which only lets it read and write its own hidden application-data folder in your Drive. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

The data travels directly between your device and Google's servers (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com, www.googleapis.com); it never passes through any server of ours. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy. What is synced: the notes, journal and other files in your vault (except the trash and anything you exclude with .hypomnemasyncignore), your configuration files, and a snapshot of your tasks and lists.

Hypomnema's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to sync your vault between your devices, is never transferred to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never read by humans.

Update checks (Windows)

The Windows app checks for new versions by downloading a small file from GitHub (github.com). Like any web request this reveals your IP address to GitHub, which handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. No personal data or app content is sent. The check runs once at startup and can be turned off in Settings > General > Updates.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data.
  • No account with us - we do not know who uses the app.

Your control

  • Stop syncing: Settings → Sync → Sign out. This revokes the app's access to your Drive and deletes the tokens from the device. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete synced data: in Google Drive, open Settings → Manage apps → Hypomnema → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete local data: delete your vault folder and uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
